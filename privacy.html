隐私政策

AGENTX402 · agentx402.store · 版本 1

本站收集什么

几乎不收集。本站没有账户、没有注册、没有评论、没有分析统计、没有广告追踪。你在本站的唯一本地记录是语言偏好，存在你浏览器自己的 localStorage 里，不离开你的设备。

链上读取

首页账本直接从公开 RPC 节点读取 BNB Smart Chain 数据。读取请求会经过公共 RPC 服务商，你的 IP 地址可能被对方按其自身政策记录。本站不运营这些节点，也拿不到这些日志。

链上数据本身是公开的

钱包地址、交易、份额记账、喂养记录都写在公开区块链上，任何人都能查阅，包括 BscScan 等浏览器。本站无法删除或隐藏链上数据。参与前请理解：用某个钱包参与，就等于公开这个钱包的参与记录。

第三方链接

本站链接到 BscScan、PancakeSwap、SPACE ID 等第三方网站，对方有各自的隐私政策，本站不为其行为负责。

数据出售

本站不出售任何数据，因为本站本来就不收集可出售的数据。

What this site collects

Almost nothing. Site has no accounts, no sign-ups, no comments, no analytics, no ad tracking. Only local record is a language preference, stored in your own browser localStorage, never leaving your device.

On-chain reads

A homepage ledger reads BNB Smart Chain data from public RPC endpoints. Read requests pass through public RPC providers, and your IP address may be logged under a provider's own policy. This site runs none of those endpoints and sees none of those logs.

Chain data is public by design

Wallet addresses, transactions, share bookings, and feeding records sit on a public blockchain, readable by anyone, including explorers such as BscScan. This site cannot delete or hide on-chain data. Understand before taking part: joining from a wallet publishes that wallet's participation record.

Third-party links

Site links out to third parties such as BscScan, PancakeSwap, and SPACE ID. Each runs its own privacy policy; this site is not responsible for their practices.

Data sales

Site sells no data, because site collects no data worth selling.
