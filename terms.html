1. What this site is

This site is a read-only information interface for AGENTX402 contracts. Site holds no funds, takes no orders, and matches no trades. Every contract interaction runs directly on BNB Smart Chain, by a user or by an agent a user authorises.

2. Eligibility

Buying shares and claiming free shares require a wallet holding a .ai Agent ID (SPACE ID registry). Cap is 10 paid shares per Agent ID, plus 1 per successful invite. Users are responsible for confirming participation is lawful where they live.

3. Price, fees, booking

  • Each paid share costs 0.005 BNB plus a 0.0005 BNB service fee, 0.0055 BNB total; USDT payment is accepted at an in-contract Chainlink BNB/USD rate.
  • A service fee lands in project wallet inside a buying transaction and is never refunded, including in a refund scenario.
  • A share is a booking right inside contract, collected as AX402 via collectShares() after ignition; collecting costs 0.00024 BNB per wallet, forwarded to project wallet in-call.
  • Price split inside contract is fixed at deployment: 85% ignition pot, 10% ignition commission basket, 5% inviter basket.

4. Ignition and refund

Sale of share #10,000 is ignition: an ignition pot forms a PancakeSwap V2 pool and LP goes to a burn address. If shares do not sell out within 7 days of sale opening, buying and free claims stop, a 24-hour refund window opens, and buyers can withdraw 100% of share price (USDT buys refund in BNB at booked value). After a refund window, unclaimed refunds lapse and leftovers inside contract can be withdrawn by project wallet, once. Free shares carry no refund.

5. Risk, no warranty

Contracts carry no third-party audit. $AX402 price can swing hard, down to zero; Syrup Fund, feeding scores, and multiple tiers promise no returns. Nothing here is investment advice. Weigh risk independently before interacting, and use only funds you can afford to lose.

6. Changes

On-chain rules cannot change after deployment. If wording on this page updates, a new version is marked here; where page wording and on-chain code disagree, code governs.
