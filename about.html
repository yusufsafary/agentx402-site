What this is

AGENTX402 is a share sale on BNB Smart Chain, for a token called $AX402. Sale is open to wallets holding a .ai Agent ID only, and runs on booked shares: 10,000 paid shares of 100,000 AX402 each, plus 1,000 free shares of 10,000 AX402 each. When share #10,000 sells, contract seeds a PancakeSwap V2 pool from an accumulated ignition pot (42.5 BNB + 850,000,000 AX402) and sends LP tokens to a burn address.

A whole sale is presented in x402 payment language: a server answers 402 Payment Required, an agent pays, contract books shares, and an on-chain record is a receipt. This site is read-only, holds no funds, and offers no web checkout; all buying runs through a user's own agent, following public docs.

How it runs

Rules live in public contracts: no owner, no pause switch, no blacklist, no upgrade proxy. Service fees, ignition commission, and sell tax land in a public project wallet, 0xBcD080905a051BcC33CbeDCa99FF1a5096e3450d, checkable by anyone on BscScan. Project belongs to no company or foundation; public channels are this site and contracts alone.
